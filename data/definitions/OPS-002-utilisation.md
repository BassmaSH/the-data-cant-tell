# Utilisation

## 1. Definition

1.1 **Utilisation** tells us how much of the fleet is out with customers. It is the share of the time the fleet could have been on rent in which it really was on rent.

1.2 Formula: utilisation = on-rent days ÷ available fleet days, stated as a percentage with one decimal. Both figures are defined in OPS-001 and must cover the same weeks, depots and categories.

1.3 Example: a depot has 20 generators for a full week, so 140 available fleet days. They were out for 98 days in total. Utilisation is 98 ÷ 140 = 70.0 %.

## 2. Rules for calculating it

2.1 For more than one week, depot or category, add up the on-rent days, add up the available fleet days, then divide. Do not average percentages.

2.2 On-rent days from every channel are added together first. There is no utilisation "of the online channel", because the fleet is not divided between channels.

2.3 Utilisation cannot be above 100 %. A higher figure means the fleet days were counted for fewer depots or weeks than the on-rent days, or the on-rent days were counted more than once.

2.4 For the week still in progress, available fleet days use the days covered so far.¹

## 3. Targets

3.1 Depot managers are measured against the targets below. Summer is fiscal weeks 1 to 26, winter is fiscal weeks 27 to 52 (CAL-001).

| Category | Summer target | Winter target |
|---|---|---|
| Access | 68 % | 54 % |
| Earthmoving | 68 % | 52 % |
| Power tools | 46 % | 36 % |
| Power and lighting | 64 % | 60 % |
| Concrete and compaction | 66 % | 50 % |

3.2 Power tools have a lower target because many small units are kept so that a customer never finds the shelf empty.

3.3 A depot is rated per category and season:

| Rating | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Green | at or above target |
| Amber | up to 8 percentage points below target |
| Red | more than 8 percentage points below target |

## 4. Reading it

4.1 Utilisation falls when fewer units are out, and also when units are added to the fleet. Check both before describing a change.

4.2 Utilisation says nothing about price. A depot can raise utilisation by renting cheaply.

---

¹ No other week is shortened. A depot that closes part-way through a week is still counted for the full seven days of that week.
