# Utilisation of the fleet

## 1. Definition

1.1 **Utilisation** tells us how hard the money invested in the fleet is working. It compares what a group of equipment earned in a year with what that equipment cost to buy.

1.2 Formula: utilisation = rental revenue of the last 52 weeks ÷ average fleet cost over the same 52 weeks, stated as a percentage with one decimal.

1.3 Rental revenue is defined in REV-001. Use one definition for all 52 weeks: the one in force in the last of them, with earlier weeks restated.

1.4 Fleet cost is the purchase cost of the units, without any reduction for age. The average is taken over the 52 weekly values.

1.5 Example: a depot's access equipment cost EUR 960,000 to buy and earned EUR 518,400 of rental revenue in the last 52 weeks. Utilisation is 518,400 ÷ 960,000 = 54.0 %.

## 2. Rules for calculating it

2.1 Always use 52 weeks. A shorter period must be scaled to a year (revenue of 13 weeks × 4), and the answer must say that it was scaled. Scaling a summer quarter overstates the year; scaling a winter quarter understates it.

2.2 For more than one depot or category, add up the revenue, add up the fleet cost, then divide.

2.3 A provisional week (DAT-001) may be included, with a remark.

2.4 Utilisation can be above 100 %: cheap equipment that is rented often earns more than its cost in a year.

## 3. Review thresholds

3.1 Finance reviews each category in each depot once a year against the thresholds below.

| Category | Review if below | Expected range |
|---|---|---|
| Access | 40 % | 45 % to 60 % |
| Earthmoving | 45 % | 50 % to 70 % |
| Power tools | 50 % | 55 % to 85 % |
| Power and lighting | 40 % | 45 % to 65 % |
| Concrete and compaction | 40 % | 45 % to 65 % |

3.2 Equipment below its threshold for two reviews in a row is proposed for sale or for transfer to another depot.

## 4. Reading it

4.1 Utilisation rises when prices rise, when more equipment is rented, or when equipment is sold. It falls when new equipment is bought, even if nothing else changes.

4.2 Because it is built on revenue, this figure can only be given to someone who is allowed to see revenue.
