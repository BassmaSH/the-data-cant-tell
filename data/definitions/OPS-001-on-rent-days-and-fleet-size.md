# On-rent days, contracts and fleet size

## 1. On-rent days

1.1 A unit is one piece of equipment that can be rented on its own: one excavator, one scaffold tower, one generator, one breaker.

1.2 An **on-rent day** is one unit being out with a customer for one calendar day. Ten units out for three days are 30 on-rent days.

1.3 Days are counted from the day the unit is handed over to the day it comes back, both included. Saturdays and Sundays count.¹

1.4 On-rent days are not the same as the days the customer pays for. A seven-day hire gives seven on-rent days but is charged as fewer days (PRC-001).

1.5 On-rent days can be added up across weeks, depots, categories and channels.

## 2. Contracts

2.1 A contract is one rental agreement with one customer. It can cover several units.

2.2 The weekly figure "contracts" counts every contract that had at least one unit out during the week. A contract that runs over three weeks is counted in each of the three weeks.

2.3 For that reason **contracts must not be added up across weeks** to give the number of contracts in a month or a year: the sum counts long contracts several times. Within one week, contracts can be added across depots, categories and channels.

## 3. Fleet size and available fleet days

3.1 **Fleet size** is the number of units a depot owns in a category at the end of the week, whether they are out, in the yard or in the workshop.²

3.2 **Available fleet days** are fleet size multiplied by the number of days the week covers.

| Fleet size (units) | Days covered by the week | Available fleet days |
|---|---|---|
| 20 | 7 | 140 |
| 20 | 3 | 60 |
| 45 | 7 | 315 |

3.3 A week normally covers seven days. The week that is still running when the figures are produced covers fewer (CAL-001, section 5).

3.4 Fleet belongs to a depot and a category. It does not belong to a channel: the same generator can go out over the counter on Monday and to a contract customer on Thursday. Fleet figures therefore exist per week, depot and category only, and must be added up once per depot and category, not once per channel.

3.5 Fleet size comes from the fleet system, not from the weekly rental files of the depots. It is present even for a week whose rental file is missing.

## 4. Fleet cost

4.1 Fleet cost is the purchase cost of the units in the fleet, in euros, without any reduction for age.

---

¹ A unit that a contract customer has asked us to "stand down" (leave on site, unused, during a site shutdown) is not on rent for those days.

² Units sent to the central yard for sale are removed from fleet size on the day they leave the depot.
