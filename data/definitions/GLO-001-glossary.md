# Glossary of reporting terms

Short meanings only. The document named in the last column is the definition; where the two differ, the document wins.

| Term | Short meaning | Defined in |
|---|---|---|
| Account | an arrangement under which a customer rents on agreed terms and is invoiced monthly | SAL-001 |
| Active customer | a contract-account customer with a rental in the recent past | SAL-002 |
| Ancillary revenue | delivery and collection fees, since April 2026 | REV-001 |
| As-of date | the last day covered by the reporting database | CAL-001 |
| Available fleet days | fleet size multiplied by the days in the week | OPS-001 |
| Average daily rate | rental revenue divided by on-rent days | REV-002 |
| Booked | final, no longer changing | DAT-001 |
| Category | the kind of equipment: Access, Earthmoving, Power tools, Power and lighting, Concrete and compaction | SAL-001 |
| Channel | how a rental was arranged: counter, online booking or contract accounts | SAL-001 |
| Charged days | the days a customer pays for, fewer than the days on rent for long hires | PRC-001 |
| Contract | one rental agreement; also used for the standing agreement with a key account | OPS-001 |
| Credit note | an amount given back to a customer | REV-001 |
| Damage waiver | optional charge limiting what a customer pays for accidental damage | REV-001 |
| Depot | a site that holds equipment and rents it out | none |
| Driver | the part of a breakdown that accounts for a change | RPT-001 |
| Fiscal year | April to March, named after the year in which it ends | CAL-001 |
| Fleet cost | purchase cost of the units in the fleet | OPS-001 |
| Fleet size | number of units a depot owns in a category | OPS-001 |
| Four-day week | charging four days for a seven-day hire | PRC-001 |
| Key account | a large customer with a negotiated contract | SAL-001 |
| Like for like | a comparison limited to depots that traded in both periods | RPT-001 |
| List price | the price of one day before discount | PRC-001 |
| Load log | the record of which depot files were loaded | DAT-001 |
| Mix | how a total is spread over categories or channels | REV-002 |
| Off-hire | the moment a customer tells us the equipment can be collected | none |
| On-rent day | one unit out with a customer for one day | OPS-001 |
| Percentage point | the unit for a change in a percentage | SAL-001 |
| Provisional | not yet final, expected to rise | DAT-001 |
| Region | North, South, East or West; each depot belongs to one | none |
| Rental revenue | what the company earned from renting equipment | REV-001 |
| Segment | the kind of customer: household, trade or key account | SAL-001 |
| Share | a part divided by its total | SAL-001 |
| Unit | one piece of equipment that can be rented on its own | OPS-001 |
| Walk-in channels | counter and online booking together | SAL-001 |
| Week | Monday to Sunday, named by its Monday | CAL-001 |

## Notes

1. "Hire" and "rental" mean the same thing in every document.

2. "Utilisation" appears in the reports of more than one department. This glossary gives no short meaning for it; ask which report the figure comes from.

3. "Revenue" without a qualifier means rental revenue.
