# Price list, charged days and discounts

## 1. The price list

1.1 The price list gives, for each region and each equipment category, the price of **one day** of rental before any discount and before tax. This is the list price.

1.2 Prices differ by region. A price is not reproduced in this document, because it changes; the price list itself is the only source.

1.3 A price change always starts on a Monday. The old price keeps its row in the price list with the last day on which it applied; the new price gets a new row with its start date. To find the price for a given day, take the row whose dates contain that day. The row with no end date is the current price, which is not the right one for a past period.

## 2. Charged days

2.1 A customer does not pay the list price for every day of a long hire. The number of days charged depends on the length of the hire.

| Length of hire | Days charged |
|---|---|
| 1 day | 1 |
| 2 days | 2 |
| 3 days | 3 |
| 4 to 7 days | 4 |
| each further week | 4 |
| weekend, Saturday morning to Monday morning | 1.5 |

2.2 This is known as the four-day week: a hire of seven consecutive days is charged as four list-price days.¹

2.3 The equipment is still on rent for all seven days (OPS-001). This is the main reason why the average daily rate is well below the list price.

## 3. Discounts

3.1 Discounts are a percentage taken off the hire charge after the charged days have been worked out.

| Customer or channel | Discount |
|---|---|
| Counter, no account | none |
| Online booking | 5 % |
| Household contract account | 5 % |
| Trade contract account | 12 % |
| Key account | negotiated per contract, typically 20 % to 25 % |

3.2 Discounts are not added together. An account customer who books online receives the account discount only.

3.3 Discounts for account customers are a percentage of the list price, so a change of list price changes what account customers pay from the same Monday.

## 4. Delivery and collection

4.1 Delivery and collection is charged at a flat fee of EUR 45.00 per contract, covering both journeys, in every region.²

4.2 The fee is not a hire charge. Where it is reported is set out in REV-001.

---

¹ The four-day week does not apply to Earthmoving. Earthmoving is charged five days for a hire of five to seven days and for each further week.

² No discount of section 3 applies to the delivery and collection fee.
