# Active customer

## 1. Definition

1.1 An **active customer** is a customer with a contract account who had at least one on-rent day in the 13 weeks that end with the reporting week.¹

1.2 The 13 weeks include the reporting week itself. For the week that starts on 29 June 2026, the window runs from the week of 6 April 2026 to the week of 29 June 2026.

1.3 A customer whose contract has ended stops being active on the end date, even if the customer still had on-rent days inside the last 13 weeks.

## 2. Who can be counted

2.1 Only contract-account customers are identified in the reporting database (SAL-001, section 4.2). The number of active customers is therefore a number of contract accounts.

2.2 There is no count of active customers for the counter channel or for online booking, and none can be worked out from the weekly figures. A request for "active online customers" cannot be answered from the reporting database.

## 3. Counting rules

3.1 Each customer is counted once, however many depots, categories or weeks the customer rented in.

3.2 **Active customers must not be added up.** The same customer is active in many weeks and may rent in several depots. The table shows one small example.

| | Depot A | Depot B | Both depots |
|---|---|---|---|
| Active customers | 14 | 9 | 21 |

Two customers rent from both depots, so the two depots together have 21 active customers, not 23.

3.3 To compare two dates, count each date separately with its own 13-week window. Do not subtract weekly counts from each other.

## 4. By segment

4.1 Active customers can be split by segment. A customer has exactly one segment, so the segments add up to the total for the same scope.

4.2 Key accounts are few. A change of one key account is a large percentage change; give the count, not only the percentage.

## 5. Related figures

5.1 "New customer" means an account opened in the period. "Lost customer" means a contract ended in the period, or an account that was active at the previous date and is not active now. The reporting database records the date a contract ended. It does not record the reason.

---

¹ For household accounts the window is 26 weeks instead of 13, because households rent rarely.
