# Fiscal calendar and week numbering

## 1. Weeks

1.1 A reporting week runs from Monday to Sunday. A week is named by the date of its Monday. Every figure in the reporting database is weekly; there are no daily figures.

## 2. Fiscal year

2.1 The fiscal year runs from April to March and is **named after the calendar year in which it ends**. Fiscal year 2027 starts in April 2026. Quarters are fiscal too: "Q1" never means January to March.

2.2 Fiscal week 1 is the first week whose Monday falls on or after 1 April. A week belongs to the fiscal year of its Monday; the days of early April that come before that first Monday belong to the last week of the previous fiscal year.

| Fiscal year | Week 1 starts | Last week starts | Number of weeks |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2026 | 7 April 2025 | 30 March 2026 | 52 |
| 2027 | 6 April 2026 | 29 March 2027 | 52 |

## 3. Quarters

| Quarter | Fiscal weeks | Roughly |
|---|---|---|
| Q1 | 1 to 13 | April to June |
| Q2 | 14 to 26 | July to September |
| Q3 | 27 to 39 | October to December |
| Q4 | 40 to 52 | January to March |

## 4. Months

4.1 A week belongs to the month of its Monday. A month therefore has **four or five weeks**, and month totals are not directly comparable.

| Month | Weeks in fiscal 2026 (2025-26) | Weeks in fiscal 2027 (2026-27) |
|---|---|---|
| April | 4 | 4 |
| May | 4 | 4 |
| June | 5 | 5 |
| July | 4 | 4 |
| August | 4 | 5 |
| September | 5 | 4 |
| October | 4 | 4 |
| November | 4 | 5 |
| December | 5 | 4 |
| January | 4 | 4 |
| February | 4 | 4 |
| March | 5 | 5 |

4.2 A five-week month is about a quarter larger than a four-week month with no change in the business. When two months with a different number of weeks are compared, compare the average per week, or say that the number of weeks differs.

## 5. Relative dates

5.1 Every relative expression is worked out from the **as-of date** of the reporting database, which is the last day the data covers. It is not worked out from the day on which the question is asked.

5.2 "This week" is the week that contains the as-of date. It usually covers fewer than seven days. "Last week" is the week before it. "Last month" is the calendar month before the month of the as-of date.

5.3 "The same week last year" is the week with the same fiscal week number in the previous fiscal year. It starts exactly 364 days earlier, on a Monday. It is not the week containing the same calendar date.
